After [Flint 40B at 120k](/en/posts/flint-40b-moe-6gb-vram/) on this same laptop, the opposite goal: a model that fits entirely on the GPU with long context. The GPU is a 6 GB **RTX 3050 Laptop**, which is also the desktop's primary GPU: the full 6 GB are never free.

## Summary

- **LFM3-1.7B is the only one of the four models that fits at 200k and still works**: 4.3-4.5 GB VRAM, tg 35-40 tok/s at 200k, needle 59/60 and tool calling 140/140 across its four versions.
- **The official Q6_K is the best pick**: highest tg (101 tok/s) and fastest agent. The abliterated variants neither lose nor gain anything measurable.
- **CUDA, not Vulkan**: ~8x better pp and no frozen desktop.
- **Thinking always on**, with a token budget sized to the task.
- **Read-only subagent: yes. Editing code: no.**

## LFM3 speed (CUDA)

| Depth | Official Q6 pp / tg | AD pp / tg | Official Q8 pp / tg | Heretic pp / tg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5183 / **101** | 5107 / 90 | 5803 / 81 | 5784 / 80 |
| 8k | 4598 / 95 | 4678 / 87 | 5138 / 77 | 5001 / 76 |
| 32k | 3466 / 81 | 3547 / 74 | 3606 / 66 | 3648 / 66 |
| 64k | 2567 / 67 | 2593 / 63 | 2721 / 57 | 2681 / 57 |
| 128k | 1676 / 50 | 1690 / 47 | 1745 / 44 | 1745 / 44 |
| 200k | 1231 / **40** | 1171 / 38 | 1262 / 36 | 1260 / 35 |

Full 200k ingest: ~1.5 min. VRAM at 200k: 4.3-4.5 GB. At the same quantization, the official Q6_K generates 10 % faster than AD; official Q8_0 and Heretic are equivalent.

### `-ub`

With no experts on the CPU there is no PCIe traffic to amortize (unlike Flint). pp4096, measured with AD and Heretic:

| pp4096 (tok/s) | ub 512 | ub 1024 | ub 2048 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AD, empty | 5134 | **5243** | 5188 |
| AD, 128k | 1705 | **1757** | 1710 |
| Heretic, empty | 5683 | **5925** | 5466 |
| Heretic, 128k | 1758 | **1772** | 1702 |

1024 wins by 2-4 %. `-ub 4096` OOMs at 128k.

> [!FAIL]
> The first `-ub` sweep used the default `-p 512`: with a 512-token prompt the batch never exceeds 512 and every option looks the same. The prompt must be longer than the batch.

## CUDA vs Vulkan (same GPU)

Equivalent tg; pp ~8x worse on Vulkan (13 min vs 1.5 min for 200k). With the dGPU as the compositor's primary, Vulkan at 100 % freezes the desktop; CUDA does not.

> [!NOTE]
> This comparison used two different builds (b12263 CUDA and b12608 Vulkan). I did not repeat it on the single build: the gap leaves no doubt.

## Thinking

Reasoning tokens by task:

| Task | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| Retrieve one fact | ~150 |
| Tool calling / agent | 100-1400 |
| Enumerate over a long text | ~6600 |
| Summarize a conversation | ~11,600 |

With a low `max_tokens`, the last two exhaust the budget without emitting `content`. Fix: up to 16k plus an instruction to reason briefly. `enable_thinking=false` is not an option: on an extraction over 85k tokens it went from 7 to 2 min, but the output was broken.

### Loops in free-form prose

10 runs per combination:

| Prose loops (out of 10) | Official Q6 | Official Q8 | AD | Heretic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| temp 0.1 + system | 1 | 2 | **0** | 1 |
| temp 0.1 no system | 3 | **0** | 2 | 2 |
| temp 0.6 + system | 3 | 3 | 2 | **0** |
| temp 0.6 no system | 1 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

JSON: 0 loops and 160/160 valid. The loops show up equally in the official release, so they come from the model, not from the abliteration. No stable optimal temperature at n=10; the system prompt helps in most cases.

## Rivals

Hybrids or models with few attention layers that, on paper, fit in 6 GB at 128k. Same engine and tests.

### tg (tok/s)

| Depth | LFM3 official Q6 | LFM3 official Q8 | Jamba 3B | Nemotron 4B | Qwen3.5 4B* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **101** | 81 | 72 | 56 | 49 |
| 32k | **81** | 66 | 70 | 47 | 26 |
| 64k | 67 | 57 | **69** | 41 | 17 |
| 128k | 50 | 44 | **66** | OOM | OOM |
| 200k | 40 | 36 | **64** | OOM | OOM |

\*Community distill on Qwen3.5-4B; KV `-ctk q8_0 -ctv q4_0`, the only way it fits.

Jamba (2 attention layers out of 28) barely degrades with depth: 72 → 64 tok/s, versus 101 → 40 for LFM3.

### Quality

| Model | Needle 8k | 32k | ~64k | 128k-200k | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LFM3 (4 versions)** | 11/12 | 12/12 | 12/12 | 24/24 | **35/35** each |
| Qwen3.5 4B (distill) | 3/3 | 3/3 | 3/3 | OOM | **35/35** (agent 9 s) |
| Nemotron 3 Nano 4B | 3/3 | 0/3 | 0/3 | OOM | 28/35 (parallel 0/5) |
| Jamba Reasoning 3B | 3/3 | 1/3 | 0/3 | 0/6 | 24/32 (irrelevance and agent 0) |

- **Jamba** degenerates from 32-64k on (broken `<think>` tags, repeated line breaks, unrelated JavaScript). Re-run with AI21's sampling (temp 0.6, no penalty): same result. Model or llama.cpp support: this data cannot separate the two.
- **Nemotron** returns empty `content` from 32k on after a few words of reasoning: likely a template or support issue. Inconclusive.
- **Qwen3.5-4B (distill)** matches LFM3's quality where it fits, but in 6 GB it stops at ~64k, where it generates at 17 tok/s.

> [!WARNING]
> Theoretical VRAM estimates put Nemotron and Qwen3.5 within 6 GB at 128k. With the compositor on the same card, neither started.

## Real use

- **Coding agent with 200k** ([pi](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi)): loads in 3 s; reads persistent memory read-only. It retrieved a decision made hours earlier, with its citation, in 12 s.
- **Search subagent** (answers with `file:line`): 2 min 25 s on CUDA vs 12 min 29 s on the iGPU; 6/6 citations correct.
- **Pre-digesting logs** for a cloud model: accurate but slow on large inputs (85k tokens: 2/2 lines, 7 min, almost all thinking).

> [!FAIL]
> As a subagent that edits code it does not work: renaming a constant across four files, it introduced a typo in the definition (broken import) and **reported the test as passing**. Liquid already advises against it for agentic coding; it is now restricted to read-only tasks.

## Server

```bash
llama-server -m LFM3-1.7B-Q6_K.gguf \
  --device CUDA0 -ngl 99 -fa on -c 204800 -np 1 -ub 1024 \
  --cache-ram 2048 --no-context-shift \
  --jinja --reasoning on \
  --temp 0.1 --top-k 50 --repeat-penalty 1.1 \
  --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8091
```

- Without `--device`, llama.cpp splits across the dGPU and the iGPU.
- No `--reasoning-budget` (not trained for it); raise `max_tokens`.
- With an explicit `-np N` you need `--kv-unified`, or each slot gets `ctx/N`.
- If you need no refusals, the abliterated variants perform the same in these tests.

Results hold for these exact llama.cpp and GGUF versions; Jamba and Nemotron could change with better support.
